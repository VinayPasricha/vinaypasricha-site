Website analytics

—
What's tracked — and what each label means

Traffic · every page, anonymous

Page view
Each time someone opens or navigates to a page (including in-app view changes inside the runtimes).
Unique visitor
A distinct browser/device, counted by a random anonymous id kept on their device. Not a person.
Session
One visit. It ends after 30 minutes of inactivity, then a new session starts.
Time on page
Seconds spent on a page, measured when the visitor leaves or switches away.
Bounce rate
Share of sessions that viewed only a single page before leaving.

Interactions · events on every page

Internal link click link
Clicked a link that stays on this site.
Outbound link click outbound_link
Clicked a link that leaves for another website.
Button click button
Clicked a button or button-like control.
Email / phone link contact_link
Clicked a mailto: or tel: link.
Form submitted form_submit
Submitted any form. We record which form and the number of fields — never the values typed in.
Scroll depth depth_25…100
Reached 25%, halfway, 75%, or the end of a page.
Rage click
3+ rapid clicks in the same spot — a signal of frustration or a broken control.
Custom event
Anything a runtime chooses to report via vpTrack('name').

Acquisition & audience · per visit

Source / campaign
Where the visit came from — a UTM tag (utm_source/utm_campaign) if present, else the referring site, else "direct". First-touch source is remembered per person.
Landing & exit page
The first page a session arrived on and the last one before leaving.
Country
Approximate, derived from the browser's timezone — we never do IP geolocation.
Browser / OS
Read from the user-agent (Chrome, Safari, Windows, iOS…).
New vs returning
Whether it's a device's first-ever visit or a repeat one.
Engaged time
Seconds a visitor was active on a page (moving/scrolling/typing) — not just idle with the tab open.

People · only after someone identifies

Known person
A visitor stops being anonymous when they: submit a lead form, share their email in an AI chat, log into the Participant Room, or open an ABL course session.
Timeline
Their full activity history — including the pages they viewed before they identified, stitched together by their device.
Profile kept
Name, email, company, phone, role, how they were identified (source), device count, first & last seen.

Privacy · what we don't do

No raw IP, no cookies
The IP is turned into a salted hash and immediately discarded; identity uses local storage, not cookies.
No form contents
Form values, passwords, and message text are never captured — only that a form was submitted.
Opt-out respected
Honors browser Do-Not-Track and a per-visitor opt-out; the Studio itself is never tracked.
