Studio / ICP & Targets / Public Intelligence

Turn a selected company — and only its URL — into a public frequency profile.

This engine consumes the 3A research queue and nothing else: only companies you selected are researched. From a company's URL it assembles publicly available signals, estimates an Organizational Frequency across twelve dimensions, maps contradictions, and drafts an SEO-ready public profile page — written carefully, in probabilistic language, never as final truth. The page is marked ready for review; it is never auto-published, and no stakeholder is ever contacted here.

Honest by construction. There is no live web, search, or social fetch in this environment — starting research with no provider blocks honestly and fabricates nothing. Manual import of public source text is the working path; with only manual material the profile is built and labelled “Based on limited public material.” Signals are extracted from real text and quote what they matched. The page carries no numeric ratings and no defamatory language — it surfaces what public signals suggest, and what still needs validation. Stakeholder validation, outreach and publishing are out of scope for this build.
