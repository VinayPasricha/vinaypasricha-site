Studio / World Discovery

The world emits signals before anyone sends a mandate.

The runtime shifts from waiting for a brief to watching for the pattern — hiring surges, reposted roles, funding, leadership change, deteriorating reviews. Each becomes a traceable signal, then a company opportunity, then a mission hypothesis. Researched opportunities flow into Organizational Intelligence; only then is an outreach draft written — and never sent automatically.

Honest by construction. There is no live web search, no LinkedIn scraping, and no funding-data feed in this environment — a scan reports that plainly and processes only the real material you import. Signal detection is a deterministic lexicon that quotes the sentence it matched. The default policy requires review; no outreach is ever sent autonomously. World Discovery detects opportunity — Intelligence interprets the organization and Search finds evidence; this layer duplicates neither.
