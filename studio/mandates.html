Understanding, turned into executable intent.

The Mission-to-Mandate Runtime. Organizations do not buy frequency — they buy outcomes. A mandate converts Mission → Gap → Role into something that can be acted on. Two journeys: a free Fast Lane to begin quickly, and a paid Deep Lane to understand before acting. A mandate answers why this role exists before who should fill it — that order is constitutional. No sourcing, no candidates, no matching.

The bridge from understanding to execution. A mandate is generated from a defined role, its purpose, the frequency gap it closes and the mission — never from a person. The Fast Lane rests on Tier-0 research and a basic frequency read; the Deep Lane proposes deep organizational discovery (founder, hiring-manager, supervisor and skip-level interviews) whose runtime arrives in Build 1K — here it is only a hook. Memory stays gated: this writes only Mandate, Success Definition, Mission Contribution and Lane Selection — never Essence, Transformation, Resonance, or any Frequency.
