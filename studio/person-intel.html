Who is this person, professionally?

Build a serious Person Intelligence Brief from real material you provide — resume, LinkedIn text, articles, posts, press, portfolio. Every signal quotes its source; every hypothesis traces to signals; every confidence states its reasoning. Then prepare 10 professional references with explicit consent and hypothesis-driven questions. No matching, ranking, fit score, or automated outreach — and only professional behaviour from professional evidence.

Live web search & LinkedIn scraping · NOT IMPLEMENTED — paste real text / links below. The runtime never pretends to have searched.

Honest by construction. The runtime reasons over the material you paste — it does not scrape LinkedIn or search the web. Résumé-only briefs are labelled weak; confidence rises with articles, press, and validated references. References are prepared with explicit, stored consent; contacting them across website / email / WhatsApp / voice is a hook for a later build — nothing is sent automatically.
