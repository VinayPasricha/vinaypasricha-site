Studio / ICP & Company Selection

Decide who matters before spending a minute researching them.

Today, research begins before we know whether a company is worth it. This console reverses that: Product → ICP → Target Universe → Discovery → Human Selection → Research Queue. An agent interviews you to define the ideal customer, generates a target universe, gathers candidate companies, and lays them out for you to select. Research begins only after a company is Marked For Research — and that happens in Build 3B, not here.

Honest by construction. There is no live Apollo, LinkedIn, public-search or job-board fetch in this environment — a discovery run with no provider configured blocks honestly and fabricates nothing. Manual import (CSV, Excel paste, Apollo export, or a pasted list) is the working path and the launch path. This layer only identifies and selects targets; it performs no deep research, no organizational frequency, and no outreach. Mark For Research queues a company for the Build 3B research runtime — nothing more.
