— What this is

The topbar of every page on the site shows a small rotating line — Now: Writing The Signal · Building GoodSpace AI · Running Cohort III. Each item is a verb + subject pair. They cycle on a slow loop. Add, remove, or reorder them here.

2 to 5 items reads best. Single-word verb + short subject. Keep it current — what is actually happening this week, not what you wish was happening.
