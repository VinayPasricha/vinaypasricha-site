An interview is validation, not just evaluation.

Public research raised hypotheses; the interview answers them. Paste a transcript and the runtime extracts first-party evidence, validates the person's frequency through the gated path, logs every change as an evolution event, preserves contradictions, and marks resonance stale for regeneration. An offer may only be drafted against fresh resonance, needs employer approval, and is never accepted on the candidate's behalf.

Honest by construction. Calendar / video / recording / transcription are provider hooks (NOT IMPLEMENTED) — scheduling and transcript are manual; no fake recordings. Raw transcript never updates frequency — only validated interview understanding does, with an evolution event and preserved history. A candidate's observations about the organization never touch org frequency directly; they become mediated learning signals. No scores, no rankings, no autonomous offers, no acceptance on the candidate's behalf. Joins open in Hiring.
