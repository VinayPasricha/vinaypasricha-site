Studio / Outreach

Discover candidates. Draft outreach. You approve.

AI-assisted candidate discovery after a mandate — with a recruiter in control before anything leaves the building. Open a search mission against a mandate, add a candidate (LinkedIn URL or manual), reuse Person Research for a preliminary brief + resonance screen, then generate a personalized outreach draft that explains why this person may resonate — and approve or reject it. Nothing is sent automatically.

Approval-gated by construction. Live candidate search (Goodspace DB / LinkedIn / Google / GitHub) is NOT IMPLEMENTED — a search mission is created and marked needs manual input; candidates are added by URL or by hand, researched via pasted public material, and screened by reusing the resonance engine. Outreach drafts require explicit human approval; there is no email/WhatsApp transport, so an approved draft only reaches sent_placeholder. No ranking, no score, no hire/reject, no auto-send. Future agents are placeholders only.
