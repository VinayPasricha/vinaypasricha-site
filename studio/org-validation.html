Studio / Public Intelligence / Validation

A public estimate is a start. Stakeholders make it true.

3B produced a useful — but unvalidated — public Organizational Frequency. This runtime improves its accuracy through structured stakeholder participation. It reads the public page, generates questions only from gaps (low-confidence dimensions, contradictions, unknowns, unvalidated claims) — never curiosity — records responses, moves confidence, and writes a history-preserving evolution event for every change. The aim is accuracy, not sales, recruitment, or hiring.

Accuracy, not persuasion. Every question points to a specific hypothesis drawn from the public estimate; nothing is asked out of curiosity. Contradiction is preserved as a signal, never erased — and prior confidence values are kept in history, never overwritten. Apollo / live contact discovery is honest-blocked (CSV and manual entry work); email / WhatsApp send is not wired (the manual channel lets you deliver the note yourself and log the reply). Each validated response can also write a Frequency Evolution event. No outreach campaigns, resonance, interviews, offers, scores, or ratings.
