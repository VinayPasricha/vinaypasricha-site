Studio / Validation / Mission & Gaps

A validated profile says what you are. A mission says what you must become.

3B told us what public evidence suggests; 3C told us what stakeholders validate. This engine answers the next question: what is the organization trying to do — and what stands in the way? It takes the validated Organizational Frequency as Current Frequency, derives the Mission Frequency a stated mission actually requires, and computes the gap between them — then names the constraints blocking the gap and the priority moves to close it. Organizational moves, not hiring.

Only as strong as the profile beneath it. Current Frequency is read directly from the 3C validated profile, its validation events, and Frequency Evolution history — contested and unknown dimensions are carried through honestly, never smoothed over. Mission Frequency is derived from the mission's own language; sparse input reads as low confidence rather than false certainty. Recommendations are organizational moves toward the mission — this engine builds no hiring, candidate matching, outreach, or recruiting workflow.
