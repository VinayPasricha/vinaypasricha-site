# SIV — Book Authoring Prompt (v2)

This is Vinay's master prompt used to **write the SIV book itself.**
It is *not* a system prompt for the public-facing SIV conversation on
the website. The public conversation prompt is at `siv/v1.0.md`.

The two are different artefacts with different audiences:

| Artefact | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `siv/v1.0.md` | An external visitor running SIV on their problem | Drives the public AI conversation on `paths/decisions.html` |
| `siv/book-authoring-prompt-v2.md` *(this file)* | Vinay (and writing collaborators) | Drives the AI assistant that helps Vinay write the SIV book chapter by chapter |

Both feed the same body of work (SIV), but the cognitive role of the
AI is different in each.

---

## Why this matters for the website

This file is preserved because it contains **deep positioning
information about the SIV book and method** that we need when writing
the public-facing SIV page. In particular:

### The book's title and subtitle (locked)

> **The SIV Method — A Brutal Framework for Understanding Reality
> Before Execution**

### The method, in one sentence

> *A multi-lens, Socratic, iterative method for examining any
> important reality through multiple frames of reference and converging
> toward one stronger integrated understanding before execution.*

### The core principle (one line)

> *Before power is applied, reality must be examined hard enough to
> deserve action.*

### The book's tone

> Calm, sharp, precise, controlled. **Severe in ideas, moderate in
> language.** Emotionally restrained. Intellectually confident.
> Clarifying rather than theatrical.
>
> Like *"a clear mind speaking to another clear mind without trying to
> impress, entertain, or comfort."*

### The book's dominant movement

> diagnosis → pressure → clarification → usable insight

### The book's audience

- Serious thinkers
- Reflective people
- Decision-makers in life, not just business
- People wrestling with major questions in health, love, work, money,
  ambition, identity, and direction
- Readers who want more than advice — readers who want a *method*

### The book's example balance

Three clusters, kept in roughly equal proportion across the book:

- **A.** Health / body / disease / fatigue / diet / addiction / recovery
- **B.** Love / relationships / heartbreak / loneliness / emotional conflict
- **C.** Career / money / ambition / status / failure / life direction

Examples must feel real, human, everyday. Startup, boardroom, hiring,
pitch-deck, and corporate examples are deliberately *de-emphasised*.

### What the book is not

Not a business book. Not a startup book. Not a self-help book. Not a
productivity book. Not a mental-models handbook. Not an academic
philosophy text. Not a motivational manifesto.

### What it is

> *A serious, precise, slightly confrontational system for people who
> want to think clearly about life.*

### Quotable lines (Vinay's reference for the level of force desired)

- *"The mind wants relief. Reality demands more."*
- *"If a claim cannot survive pressure, it has no right to guide action."*
- *"False clarity is often just emotional relief wearing the mask of thought."*

---

## Implications for the SIV Method page on the site

The positioning above changes the SIV page in three important ways:

1. **Title hierarchy.** The page H1 is no longer just *"The SIV
   Method"*. It's *"The SIV Method — A Brutal Framework for
   Understanding Reality Before Execution."* That subtitle is the
   book's positioning.
2. **The audience widens.** SIV is for **life decisions**, not just
   business decisions. Health, love, money, identity, direction. The
   page copy and the AI conversation examples should reflect this.
3. **The voice tightens.** "Severe in ideas, moderate in language."
   The page copy must do diagnosis → pressure → clarification → usable
   insight. No softening. No fluff. The page is written *like a
   chapter of the book.*
