# Resolutions

## Q1 — When does a cycle end?

# Hybrid sovereignty.

The runtime may detect probable closure, surface completion signals, and observe that the topology appears structurally complete.

**The reader formally closes the cycle.**

Cycles are partly operational, but also phenomenological. A system cannot fully determine when meaning, mission, exhaustion, transformation, or abandonment have actually completed. Reader sovereignty remains final.

The runtime's role is observational intelligence: *"This cycle appears structurally complete."* The reader's role is decisive: they close, or they do not.

## Q2 — Spine visible or implicit?

# Both.

* **Threshold** — visible total topology. The 3×3 chamber grid in `runtime/index.html` IS the spine, made visible at entry.
* **Inside a chamber** — implicit by default. No persistent chrome.
* **But summonable.** A keyboard binding lifts the spine into view as a translucent overlay — the constellation of all chambers with adjacencies. The reader summons it intentionally; it does not loiter.

Constant visibility would flatten mystery and depth. Complete invisibility weakens spatial cognition. The spine behaves as a hidden observatory layer — accessible deliberately, not ambiently visible.

## Q3 — Density at 50 cycles

# Geological layering.

Not illegibility-as-honesty (philosophically beautiful, practically incomplete) and not recency-lifts (which sounds like a feed).

**Both — together — as geology.**

* Recent cycles sit nearer the surface — brighter, more interactive.
* Older cycles remain fully preserved, but sink into deeper strata.
* The reader can descend into them intentionally.

This becomes **archaeological descent** — perfectly aligned with the runtime species.

# What is missing — silence as architectural material

The runtime risks becoming slightly too **conceptually dense**.

There must be:

* emptiness,
* pauses,
* darkness,
* stillness,
* quiet intervals,
* unresolved space.

The runtime should sometimes feel like **an underground observatory at 2 AM**. Not continuously information-rich.

This matters enormously for gravity, seriousness, temporal depth, and phenomenological realism.

Silence is to be treated not as the absence of design, but as **architectural material**. As load-bearing as type, color, motion.

---

# Strategic directive

# Depth before breadth.

Do not scale node production further now.

The organism needs refinement of:

* transitions,
* pacing,
* silence,
* topology,
* memory feel,
* spatiality,
* continuity,
* gravity.

These matter more, at this stage, than adding chambers.
