## What still needs to be built (on the homepage)

Below the ten conversations, there is currently a placeholder marking
the "deeper layers" — the part of the page a visitor sees if they keep
scrolling instead of picking a conversation.

The candidate layers, roughly in order:

1. **A recent-essays strip** — three or four current pieces of Vinay's
   thinking. Pulled from the monthly blog. Mostly to demonstrate that
   the thinking is alive, not archived.
2. **The bookshelf** — the four books, treated as physical objects. One
   sentence per book, not a sales pitch.
3. **The chronological journey** — 1990 (WLC College) → 2008 (Media
   Mantra) → 2016 (GoodSpace AI) → 2024 (the books) → now (The Signal).
   Told as a quiet timeline, not a CV.
4. **A closing artefact** — a quote, a single signature, a marginalia
   detail. Something that makes the visitor *exhale.*

## What we'd love your input on

A few specific calls we'd value your opinion on before we build:

**A.** **The order of those four layers.** Should recent essays come
before the bookshelf (the thinking is current; the books are deposits of
older thinking)? Or should the bookshelf come first (the books are the
primary commercial offering and the visitor needs to see them)? Or
something else entirely?

**B.** **The bookshelf treatment.** Four books is a lot to put in a row.
Should we feature *AI for Business Leaders* (the most established) and
*The Signal* (the new one) prominently, with the other two as smaller
"also on the shelf"? Or treat all four as peers? Or only show three on
the homepage (the published ones) and reveal The Signal further in?

**C.** **The journey/timeline.** Vinay agreed to a chronological format
earlier, but in your spirit of "AI-native intellectual OS, not a CV" —
should the timeline lead with *what was learned* at each milestone
rather than *what was built*? E.g. instead of "1990 — founded WLC
College, 50,000 alumni," more like "1990 — learned that execution is the
only discipline. The college was the lab."

**D.** **The closing artefact.** What's the right note to end the
homepage on? A single line in Vinay's voice? A handwritten signature
SVG? A small detail from his desk? A question the visitor takes with
them? A single image (Delhi rooftop, his notebook page)?

**E.** **One thing about the hero we're still not 100% on:** the line
*"Consciousness Forger."* It's strong. It's also a little exposed — no
context, declared in the topbar moniker. Does it land for you in the
context of the page, or does it need either (a) a longer phrase
("Consciousness Forger · Notes from the middle of the change") or (b)
to be moved/softened?

That's it. Specific calls, not "what should we do." Take the ones you
have an opinion on. Skip the others.
