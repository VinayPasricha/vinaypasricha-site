A private five-week working group for Harvard OPM alumni thinking through AI in their own business.

Not a public program. Not an open application. A private cohort for Harvard OPM alumni already on the WhatsApp list — built around the framework from AI for Business Leaders, applied to your company, your team, your constraints.

The AI Leadership Course is a private five-week working group. Alumni who already have access enter the workspace. There is no public application.

— What you do over five weeks

Six modules as the curriculum map. Five weeks of working time. One AI strategy you actually own.

A private five-week working group. Each session is a 90-minute live conversation with the cohort, a working assignment between sessions, and a deliverable you can take into your next leadership meeting. The six modules below map the work; the live program is five weeks.

i.

The AI honest snapshot

An unromantic map of what AI actually is in 2026 — what works reliably, what doesn't, and where the genuine leverage lives. We separate the marketing from the substrate.

Deliverable · your company's AI honest snapshot
ii.

Where the leverage is in your business

The structural exercise of identifying — concretely, not in slides — the two or three places where AI deployment could shift a real metric in your specific business.

Deliverable · your leverage map, prioritised
iii.

What you will not use AI for

The defensive doctrine. What kinds of decisions, customer interactions, and internal processes must stay human — and what your written commitment to that looks like.

Deliverable · your AI red lines, signed
iv.

The human-AI handoff protocol

How tasks move between AI and your team. The boundary cases. Who decides when AI escalates back to a human. How quality is measured. The protocol turns AI from a vibe into a system.

Deliverable · your team's handoff protocol
v.

Re-architecting teams and metrics

What changes inside your team when AI is a colleague. New roles, retired ones, the metrics that no longer measure anything useful, the ones that suddenly do.

Deliverable · your team redesign, draft v1
vi.

Your leadership posture

The last week is personal. What kind of leader do you want to be through this transition? What posture do you want your team to mirror? You leave with a written posture, defended.

Deliverable · your written AI leadership posture
