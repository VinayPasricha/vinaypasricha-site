— About this site

The site itself.

What is this site, exactly?

It is the home of the work — books, AI conversations, videos, the AI Leadership Course, GoodSpace AI, and the longer project of building an AI-native intellectual operating system for navigating a rapidly changing world.

The structure is deliberately quiet — a library, not a feed. If you are looking for hot takes, you are in the wrong place. If you are looking for material to think alongside, this site is built for you.

Why is it in 13 languages?

Because the ideas matter to readers across India and the world, and the language switcher should not be tokenistic. English is the source. The other twelve are produced through high-quality automatic translation, then reviewed over time. Quality varies; the team is improving it.

To switch languages, use the dropdown in the top right of any page. Your choice is remembered between visits.

Who built it?

Vinay, with help. The writing, the structure, and the visual register are Vinay's. The technical build is small and intentional — calm, library-style, no analytics theatre.

Can I share or quote material from the site?

Yes, with attribution. A short quote with a link back is welcome anywhere. For longer extracts, write to vinay@vinaypasricha.com and we will almost always say yes.

— The books

The books.

How many books are there?

Five planned in this arc. Four are published or being published in 2026: AI for Business Leaders, The SIV Method, The Execution Doctrine, and Organizational Frequency. The fifth — The Signal — is being written and is due late 2026.

See them all on the books page.

In what order should I read them?

There is no required order. That said: if you are an operator inside a company being changed by AI, start with AI for Business Leaders. If you have a hard decision in front of you, start with The SIV Method. If you are stuck on execution, start with The Execution Doctrine. If you are hiring or thinking about your own role, start with Organizational Frequency.

Are they on Amazon? Anywhere else?

Yes — Amazon is the primary channel for now. Each book page has a direct buy link. Other retailers and language editions will be added over time.

For bulk orders (cohorts, leadership teams, corporate libraries), write to vinay@vinaypasricha.com.

Are there audiobook editions?

Not yet. The first audiobook is planned for the latter half of 2026, starting with The SIV Method.

— The SIV conversation

The SIV Method conversation.

What actually happens when I try it?

You describe a decision in your own words. The AI facilitator walks you through the SIV method — multiple lenses, Socratic pressure, an integrated understanding — and produces a one-page thinking artefact you can keep. The whole thing takes four to six minutes.

Try it here.

Is the AI Vinay?

No. The AI is a facilitator trained on Vinay's method. Vinay's writing is in the books and the essays — not in the conversation. The facilitator is deliberately neutral and structured.

Is my conversation private?

Yes. Your responses are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server unless you explicitly choose to share the artefact. You can close the tab at any time and your data is gone.

Can I use SIV without the AI?

Yes — that is what the book is for. The book contains the full method in writing. The conversation is one way to use it; the worksheet at the back of the book is another. Many readers do both.

— GoodSpace AI

Hiring & GoodSpace AI.

What is GoodSpace AI?

An AI-native talent platform built around the doctrine of frequency and resonance laid out in Organizational Frequency. Instead of resume-matching, the platform helps companies discover candidates whose natural frequencies resonate with the actual environment they would be joining.

I'm hiring. Where do I start?

The Organizational Frequency page is the doctrine. The practical platform is at goodspace.ai. Or write to us for a direct introduction.

I'm looking for work. Where do I start?

The Find Work page walks you through the candidate side of the doctrine. The platform itself is free for candidates.

— The course

The AI Leadership Course.

How is the course different from the book?

The book is the framework. The course is the framework applied to your business over six weeks, with seventeen other senior operators thinking alongside you. The cohort is the point — much of the value comes from the other people in the room.

When does the next cohort start?

Cohort IV begins in Q3 2026. Full details and the application are on the course page.

Is it selective?

Yes — capped at 18 operators per cohort and selected by referral or letter. The selection is not about prestige; it is about cohort balance and the seriousness of the question you are bringing.
