The Learning Trap

  • 0:26

Summary

Vinay Pasricha imagines someone changing their diet, gym routine, sleep schedule and apps all in the same month. Six weeks later they feel better or worse but can't tell which change caused it: the result is real but the cause is invisible. Running five experiments at once, he says, doesn't teach you five things; it teaches you nothing five times. The Short ends with a pointer to The SIV Method.

Key points

  • Changing many things at once hides which one caused the result.
  • The result is real, but the cause becomes invisible.
  • Running five experiments together teaches nothing five times.

It means you learn nothing five times.
