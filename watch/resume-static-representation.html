Summary

Vinay Pasricha argues that a resume cannot say the truest thing about a person: which environments make them sharp, which teams make them quiet, and which kinds of work make them indispensable. He calls this the problem of static representation, and says it is why so many senior professionals are stuck in roles that look right on paper and feel wrong in practice. In Organizational Frequency he writes about a move to continuous understanding, a layer he says GoodSpace AI is building.

Key points

  • Resumes can't capture which environments and teams bring out your best.
  • He calls this the problem of static representation.
  • It leaves senior professionals in roles that look right on paper but feel wrong.
  • He proposes continuous understanding instead.
