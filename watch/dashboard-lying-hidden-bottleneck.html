Your Dashboard Is Lying to You

Summary

Vinay Pasricha points out that you only ever measure what you decided to measure, so every metric tells you the instrumented parts are working. The real problem sits where nobody built a tracker: the waiting, the handoff, the bottleneck nobody owns. Green numbers, he says, don't mean it's working; they mean you aren't looking at the broken part.

Key points

  • Dashboards only reflect what you chose to measure.
  • Real bottlenecks hide in waiting time, handoffs and unowned steps.
  • Green metrics can mean you're not looking at the broken part.

Green numbers don't mean it's working.
