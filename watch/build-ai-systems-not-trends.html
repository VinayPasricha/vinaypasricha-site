Summary

Vinay Pasricha argues that the companies winning with AI are not the ones with the best tools but the ones with the clearest thinking. When a leader understands where AI fits inside memory, reasoning, action and feedback, everything accelerates: decisions get faster, teams get sharper and each cycle produces more than the last. AI, he says, is not a department you build but an execution layer you design into the company itself, and that clarity is what AI for Business Leaders aims to give.

Key points

  • Clear thinking, not better tools, separates companies winning with AI.
  • Leaders should understand where AI fits in memory, reasoning, action and feedback.
  • AI is an execution layer designed into the company, not a separate department.

AI is not a department you build.
