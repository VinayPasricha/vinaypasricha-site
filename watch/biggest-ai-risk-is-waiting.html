Summary

Vinay Pasricha challenges the view that the biggest AI risk is moving too fast. He argues the opposite: the biggest risk is waiting. While a company debates policies, competitors are learning; while it evaluates tools, they are building capabilities; while it plans, they are compounding. AI rewards organisations that learn faster than everyone else, not those that wait for certainty. He positions AI for Business Leaders as a way to move forward without the hype and without the fear.

Key points

  • The bigger AI risk is waiting, not moving too fast.
  • Competitors learn, build and compound while others debate policies and evaluate tools.
  • AI rewards organisations that learn faster, not those that wait for certainty.
