India’s Fire Safety Scam

  • India & Systems
  • 13 June 2026
  • 2:30
  • English
  • Video
  • Justice Delayed

Summary

In this video from the Justice Delayed series, Vinay Pasricha looks at why fire tragedies in India follow the same script every time. The owner is blamed, a missing fire NOC is discovered, a junior officer is suspended, a committee is formed, and then everyone moves on. He argues this is not the full truth. India does not lack fire safety rules: codes, inspections and compliance checklists all exist on paper. The problem is that fire safety has become part of a larger "extraction system". The fire officer is often trapped himself: he is punished if he does not collect and isolated if he tries to stay honest. The same pattern runs through municipal approvals, building permissions and police permissions. Vinay calls the junior officer the "retail face" of corruption. The "wholesale" system sits above him, in the bureaucracy, the licensing machinery and a slow judiciary. That is why a building owner decides that if he has to pay anyway, spending lakhs on real safety isn't worth it, and so exits stay blocked and wiring stays unsafe. After a fire, he says, the theatre of arrests and suspensions leaves the senior chain untouched. His answer is not more rules but public accountability: every fire NOC online, every inspection video-recorded and every approval chain visible.

Key points

  • India already has fire safety codes, inspections and checklists. The failure is in enforcement, not in the rules.
  • Fire safety has become part of an "extraction system" that makes corruption easier than honesty, even for officers who want to be honest.
  • Junior officers are the "retail face" of corruption. The "wholesale" system sits in the bureaucracy, the licensing machinery and a slow judiciary.
  • After a tragedy, arrests and suspensions are theatre that leaves the senior chain untouched.
  • His proposed fix is public accountability: fire NOCs online, video-recorded inspections and visible approval chains.

Fire safety is not paperwork. It is life.
