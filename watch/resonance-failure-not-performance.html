Summary

Vinay Pasricha warns leaders that last quarter's best hire may already be quietly disengaging. It won't show in their output yet, but in their meeting energy. He calls this a resonance failure: not a performance issue but a frequency mismatch that couldn't be seen in the interview room. He says his book Organizational Frequency shows how to spot it before making the offer.

Key points

  • Disengagement shows in meeting energy before it shows in output.
  • What looks like a performance issue can be a resonance failure.
  • The mismatch is invisible in interviews; the book aims to catch it before the offer.

It is not a performance issue.
