जब न्याय की कीमत न्याय से अधिक हो जाए

Summary

In this Hindi video, Vinay Pasricha asks viewers to imagine that someone has seized their land. They have every document and piece of evidence, the law is on their side, and they win the case after 20 years. He questions whether that is really justice. Over those two decades they paid lawyers, made endless court visits, and watched their children grow up and their parents grow old, only for the court to confirm what they knew from the start. He argues that India's problem goes deeper than "justice delayed is justice denied". Justice does arrive, but by then its value has died, and the process itself becomes the punishment. He asks whether someone who spends 15 lakh to recover 10 lakh has won, or someone whose business collapses before the verdict, or a family that breaks apart over a property dispute. On paper one side wins, but in reality everyone loses, and the biggest loss is trust in the justice system. He compares this with a hospital that treats a patient after death or a school that grants a degree at 60, and says justice too has an expiry date. His conclusion is that India needs timely verdicts, not just more of them: justice that reaches citizens within their lifetime and produces winners, not exhausted survivors. He invites viewers to comment "Justice with deadline".

Key points

  • Winning a land case after 20 years may only be an official stamp on a truth the citizen already knew.
  • In India, the process itself becomes the punishment, and justice arrives after its value has died.
  • Spending 15 lakh to recover 10 lakh, or losing a business before the verdict, is not victory.
  • Justice has an expiry date, just as treatment after death is not medical success.
  • India needs timely verdicts, not just more verdicts: justice within a citizen's lifetime.

Hindi summary

इस वीडियो में विनय पसरीचा कहते हैं कि कल्पना कीजिए किसी ने आपकी ज़मीन पर कब्ज़ा कर लिया। आपके पास सारे कागज़ और सबूत हैं, और 20 साल बाद आप मुकदमा जीत जाते हैं। पर क्या यह सचमुच न्याय है? इन सालों में वकीलों की फ़ीस, अदालतों के चक्कर, बच्चे बड़े हो गए और माता-पिता बूढ़े हो गए, और अदालत ने बस इतना बताया कि आप शुरू से सही थे। उनका कहना है कि भारत की बीमारी "न्याय में देरी, न्याय से इनकार" से भी गहरी है: न्याय मिलता है, पर तब तक उसकी कीमत मर चुकी होती है और प्रक्रिया ही सज़ा बन जाती है। 10 लाख वापस पाने में 15 लाख खर्च हो जाएँ, या फ़ैसले से पहले धंधा खत्म हो जाए, तो क्या यह जीत है? न्याय की भी एक एक्सपायरी डेट होती है। भारत को सिर्फ़ ज़्यादा फ़ैसले नहीं, समय पर फ़ैसले चाहिए, ऐसा न्याय जो नागरिक के जीवनकाल में पहुँचे।
