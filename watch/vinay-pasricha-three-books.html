Summary

In this short introduction, Vinay Pasricha explains why he wrote his books. For years he watched smart leaders with great strategies still fail to deliver, and he says the problem is almost never the idea; it is the execution. He then introduces three books. Execution Doctrine is about closing the gap between what you decide and what actually gets done. AI for Business Leaders shows how to use AI as a force multiplier without hype or jargon. The SIV Method offers a simple framework for thinking and acting with clarity under pressure. He says the books are not theory but come from real decisions, real teams and real results, and are written for people who lead teams, run companies or build something that matters.

Key points

  • Smart leaders with good strategies often fail at execution, not ideas.
  • Execution Doctrine closes the gap between deciding and doing.
  • AI for Business Leaders treats AI as a force multiplier without hype or jargon.
  • The SIV Method is a framework for clear thinking and action under pressure.
