Summary

In this English Short, Vinay Pasricha says India is being destroyed bit by bit, not by outsiders but by its own silence. Grandfathers saw the first cracks of corruption and didn't push back hard enough; fathers, busy feeding families, adjusted and compromised on small things; his own generation built careers and businesses while the system rotted, politicians became predators and the bureaucracy became a steel cage. Now, he says, the "cockroaches" are rising: Gen Z refuses to compromise, sees the predatory system clearly and won't stay silent or adjust. He calls this the nation's hope and asks viewers to support them.

Key points

  • India is being damaged by its own silence, not by outsiders.
  • Each earlier generation adjusted and compromised as corruption took root.
  • Gen Z refuses to adjust to a predatory system.
  • He frames Gen Z's resistance as the republic's hope and calls for support.

Not by outsiders, but by our own silence.
